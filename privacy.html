Channel Studio: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Channel Studio is a private desktop application that its owner uses to manage their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, Channel Studio asks for:

  • YouTube (manage your YouTube account): to read the channel's videos and schedule, set privacy and publish times, and update titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails of the owner's own videos.
  • YouTube upload: to upload the owner's videos to their own channel.
  • YouTube Analytics (read only): to read views, watch time, impressions and click-through rate for the owner's own channel.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to provide these features to the channel owner.
  • Everything is stored locally on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept in local files and the macOS Keychain.
  • No data is sent to any server run by Channel Studio. There is none.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Channel Studio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove Channel Studio's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app and its local data folder removes everything it stored.
